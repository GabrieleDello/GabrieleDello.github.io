It was a cold yet sunny day of November when I had the pleasure of visiting the Dutch contractor [Ten Hove](https://www.tenhovekamperveen.nl/), in Kamperveen. Jacco and Ryan Ten Hove welcomed me for a very nice conversation in front of a cup of coffee.
We talked a lot, discussing about the story of their company, business perspectives, and agricultural equipment. This conversation turned out to be a nice piece on ["Il Contoterzista"](https://contoterzista.edagricole.it/contoterzista-la-rivista/), the official magazine of the Italian contractors body [CAI Agromec](http://www.caiagromec.it/).

I acknowledge Jacco and Ryan Ten Hove for their willingness to share with me the story of their family business. A special thank to Thijs Jacobsen, who made the interview possible. 

The full interview is available on the January issue.
