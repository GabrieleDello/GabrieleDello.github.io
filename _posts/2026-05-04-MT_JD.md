[John Deere](https://www.deere.com/en/) is pushing up the limits of conventional tractor design.
In my latest article for Macchine Trattori, I take a closer look at the new [8R and 8Rx series](https://www.deere.com/en/tractors/row-crop-tractors/row-crop-8-family/), currently the most powerful conventional tractor on the market, with up to 634 hp (equivalent to 473 kW) in Peak Power mode.

Some key aspects, in short:
• new eAutoPowr transmission, with electric generators to improve efficiency
• a strong focus on operator environment, with the new CommandView 4/4 Plus cabins
• high hydraulic capacity, up to 418 l/min, to handle increasingly demanding implements like planters or air drills.
