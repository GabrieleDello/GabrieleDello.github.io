"[Agritechnica](https://www.agritechnica.com/en/) is the world's leading expo for agricultural technology, hosted biannully in Hannover, Germany. In November 2025, I had the pleasure to visit it and get in touch with the latest developments for future fields. Needless to say that for me, as keen on farming technologies, it was a truly amazing experience. 

Today, we release the January 2026 [Macchine Trattori issue](https://www.macchinetrattori.info/macchine-trattori-gennaio-2026/). I wrote a piece on Agritechnica's trend, please give a look to it at page 20-22. 

The issue is in Italian, however the website is also available in English: "[Macchine Trattori - English](https://www.macchinetrattori.info/en/)"
