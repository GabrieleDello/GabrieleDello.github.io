Ineos is not only a global chemical company: it also encompasses automotive and sports interests. So we have Ineos Grenadier, the business division ventured into the automotive sector. The issue is in Italian, however the website is also available in English: "[Macchine Trattori - English](https://www.macchinetrattori.info/en/)"
