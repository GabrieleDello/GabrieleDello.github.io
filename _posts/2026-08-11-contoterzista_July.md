My latest article for *Il Contoterzista* took me to Carinthia, Austria, where I met Simon Schaller-Anderwald and discovered a family farm that has made potato production its core business.

Over the last 30 years, the farm has grown from 20–25 hectares and dairy cattle to around 350 hectares, with 170–180 dedicated to potatoes.

What impressed me most was the attention to quality throughout the entire process. From soil preparation and stone separation to harvesting, storage and optical sorting, every investment has a clear purpose: delivering a consistent product while keeping the production chain under control.

But technology is only part of the story. During our conversation, Simon repeatedly came back to another essential ingredient: **trust** — in employees, dealers, customers and the farmers who make their land available for potato production.

The piece is available here: [Schaller-Anderwald, la scommessa della qualità](https://contoterzista.edagricole.it/esperienze-di-agromeccanici/schaller-anderwald-la-scommessa-della-qualita/)


["Il Contoterzista"](https://contoterzista.edagricole.it/contoterzista-la-rivista/) is the official magazine of the Italian contractors' body [CAI Agromec](http://www.caiagromec.it/).

Many thanks to Simon and the Schaller-Anderwald family for welcoming me and sharing their story.
